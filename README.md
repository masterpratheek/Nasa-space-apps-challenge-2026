# NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026
## ASTRONAUT MISSION TRAINER

**Apollo 11 Mission Experience - Interactive Game for Ages 7-12**

Experience humanity's greatest achievement: the first steps on the Moon!

### Features
- Full Apollo 11 chronological journey
- Interactive launch preparation system checks
- Cinematic Saturn V launch animation
- Earth atmospheric layers exploration
- Multi-stage rocket visualization
- Orbital mechanics simulator
- Translunar injection demonstration
- Columbia & Eagle docking sequence
- Lunar orbit insertion
- Landing zone hazard identification challenge
- Continuous lunar or Martian outpost chapter with a 10-day resource-management simulation
- Cinematic Mission Control debrief with Apollo and outpost progress reporting
- Final Earth-and-Moon mission celebration

### Mission Timeline: July 16-20, 1969
- Launch: July 16, 1969, 9:32 AM EDT
- Landing: July 20, 1969, 4:17 PM EDT at Sea of Tranquility

### Outpost Science Sources and Simulation Notes
- [NASA DONKI archived CME record](https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/DONKI/view/CME/37629/-1) — a March 1, 2025 CME model included a predicted glancing Mars arrival on March 6. This is historical context, not a live alert or the game's simulated storm.
- [NASA Moon Facts](https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/) — lunar surface and exosphere context.
- [NASA Mars Facts](https://science.nasa.gov/mars/facts/) — planetary environment context.
- [NASA Artemis](https://www.nasa.gov/artemis/) — lunar exploration context.

Outpost resources, event timing, and outcomes are simplified educational simulations. No live NASA data or external API is required to play.
